1 · Find

Ask in plain words. Get the papers that fit your project.

Search more than 250 million scholarly works. Describe what you need; the AI plans the searches, reads what comes back and keeps what fits your project.

  • AI search or keywords: every result says why it fits, and adds to your library in one click.
  • Research radar: new papers in your field and calls for papers, each week, without searching again.
  • Open access PDFs, found for you: one click fetches a paper's legal open copy and saves it in your library.

“Find missing PDFs”: one click for the whole library

ResearchCube looks up each paper's open access copy by its DOI (OpenAlex, Unpaywall, arXiv), checks that it is really a PDF and saves it to your Google Drive. Papers without an open copy get a link to their page. Free: 5 a day; Pro: no limit.

2 · Read

Drop your PDFs. The library fills itself in.

Drag up to 20 PDFs at once: titles, authors, year and DOI are read from the files and checked against OpenAlex, with a progress bar while it works.

  • Read and annotate: highlights, notes and reading status next to the PDF, saved with the paper.
  • Ask the paper: the AI answers from the PDF you are reading and from your library.
  • Keep it in order: categories, filters, BibTeX import and export.
3 · Write

Real LaTeX in the browser. Nothing to install.

Write and compile LaTeX right in ResearchCube. Cite from your library; the bibliography is built from it on every compile.

  • Ready templates: IEEE, Elsevier, Springer, thesis and Beamer, or upload your own.
  • Spelling in English and Turkish, outline, figures (PNG, PDF, EPS) and version history.
  • AI writing help that knows your library: draft, rewrite and fix compile errors.
4 · Work together

Write with your co-authors, live.

Share a project and edit the same file at the same time. You see who is where, and every change is end-to-end encrypted on its way.

  • Suggested changes and comments: accept or reject them one by one, like in a word processor.
  • Viewers and editors: decide who can change the project and who only reads it.
  • Nothing is lost: deletes go to your Drive trash, with “Undo” right after.

Questions

Where are my files kept?

In a ResearchCube folder in your own Google Drive. Our server keeps only your account, your plan, the Drive connection and the list of people you share a project with.

I used the ResearchCube desktop app. What happens to my projects?

Nothing changes in your Drive: the web app opens the same projects (library, PDFs, reports) as they are. Sign in with the same Google account.

Can I bring my existing references and LaTeX files?

Yes. Import a BibTeX file or drop your PDFs into the library, and upload your .tex files, figures and your own template to the editor.

Which devices does it run on?

In any modern browser on Windows, Mac, Linux and phones, and as an app from the Microsoft Store. Updates arrive by themselves.

Is the open access PDF finder legal?

It only fetches copies that publishers, repositories or authors made openly available (as listed by OpenAlex, Unpaywall and arXiv). When a copy cannot be downloaded automatically, you get a link to its page.
